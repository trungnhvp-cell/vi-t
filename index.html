Band 6.0

Task 2 — Public Transport vs New Roads

271 từ 4 đoạn
▾

1Nowadays, traffic congestion is a serious problem in many cities. Some people argue that the government should spend more money on public transport instead of building new roads. In my opinion, I agree with this view because public transport is better for the environment and it can reduce traffic jams.

2Firstly, public transport is more environmentally friendly than private cars. For example, one bus can carry fifty passengers, but fifty cars need much more fuel and produce a lot of pollution. Therefore, if more people use buses and trains, the air quality will improve significantly. This is very important for the health of people living in cities.

3Secondly, investing in public transport can reduce traffic congestion. When people use trains or buses, there are fewer cars on the road. As a result, the roads are less crowded and everyone can travel faster. In addition, public transport is cheaper for citizens, especially for students and old people who cannot afford to buy a car.

4However, some people think that building new roads is also necessary. They believe that wider roads can solve traffic problems quickly. In my view, this is not a good solution because more roads attract more cars, and the problem will come back after a few years.

5In conclusion, I strongly believe that governments should invest more in public transport. This will protect the environment and reduce traffic congestion in the long term.

1
Mở bài tốt: Paraphrase đề rõ ràng và nêu quan điểm ngay. Tuy nhiên "In my opinion, I agree" bị lặp ý — chỉ cần "In my opinion, ..." hoặc "I agree ...".
2
Linking words cơ bản: Firstly, Therefore, Secondly, As a result, In addition, However, In conclusion — đủ để đạt Coherence 6.0.
3
Cần cải thiện: "very important" và "a lot of pollution" còn đơn giản. Band 6.5+ nên dùng crucial / vital / substantial amounts of pollution.
4
Đoạn nhượng bộ: Có phản biện ("However...") — đây là điểm cộng cho Task Response vì thể hiện tư duy hai chiều.
5
Kết bài: Tóm tắt được quan điểm, nhưng chưa mở rộng. Có thể thêm 1 câu khuyến nghị để đạt band cao hơn.
Band 7.0

Task 2 — Public Transport vs New Roads

294 từ 5 đoạn
▾

1It is often argued that governments should prioritise investment in public transport over the construction of new roads. While I acknowledge that road expansion can bring short-term benefits, I strongly believe that allocating a larger share of the budget to public transport is the more sustainable and equitable choice.

2The primary reason for this view is environmental. Private vehicles are a major source of carbon emissions and air pollution in urban areas. By contrast, a single bus or train can transport dozens of passengers while producing a fraction of the emissions per person. Consequently, shifting commuters from cars to public transport would substantially reduce a city's carbon footprint and improve public health.

3A second argument concerns long-term efficiency. Building new roads is often presented as a quick fix for congestion; however, evidence from numerous cities suggests that additional road capacity simply attracts more traffic — a phenomenon known as induced demand. Investing in a reliable metro or bus network, on the other hand, offers a lasting solution because it moves large numbers of people using far less space.

4Admittedly, some commuters, particularly those in rural or suburban areas, depend heavily on private cars, and for them better roads remain essential. Nevertheless, this does not justify making road construction the priority for the majority of urban residents.

5In conclusion, although new roads may offer short-term relief, I firmly believe that governments should invest primarily in public transport. Doing so would deliver environmental benefits, reduce congestion more effectively in the long run, and create a fairer transport system for all citizens.

1
Cấu trúc nhượng bộ mạnh: "While I acknowledge..., I strongly believe..." — thể hiện tư duy phản biện, đây là dấu hiệu của Task Response band 7.
2
Từ vựng học thuật: prioritise, allocating, sustainable, equitable, emissions, carbon footprint — nâng band Lexical Resource lên 7.0.
3
Câu phức đa dạng: Mệnh đề quan hệ rút gọn ("a phenomenon known as induced demand"), câu ghép với "while", "because". Tăng Grammatical Range.
4
Đoạn nhượng bộ: "Admittedly... Nevertheless..." — thừa nhận mặt trái rồi phản biện, rất hiệu quả để đạt band 7.
5
Kết bài ba lớp: Khẳng định quan điểm + liệt kê 3 lợi ích cụ thể. Tránh được lỗi "kết bài lặp lại mở bài".
Band 6.5

Task 1 — Bar Chart: Transport Modes

196 từ 5 đoạn
▾

1The bar chart compares the percentage of commuters using five different modes of transport in a city in 2000 and 2020.

2Overall, the car was the most popular means of transport in both years, although its share declined considerably. By contrast, the proportions of people using trains and bicycles increased noticeably over the period.

3In 2000, just over half of commuters (52%) travelled by car, making it by far the dominant mode. However, by 2020 this figure had fallen to 38%. The proportion of people using buses remained relatively stable, rising slightly from 24% to 26%.

4Train use saw the most significant growth, climbing from 14% in 2000 to 22% in 2020, a rise of eight percentage points. Similarly, the percentage of commuters cycling to work almost doubled, from 6% to 11%. In contrast, the proportion of people walking fell marginally, from 4% to 3%, making it the least common method of transport in both years.

5In summary, while cars remained the most widely used form of transport, the period witnessed a clear shift towards more sustainable alternatives such as trains and bicycles.

1
Câu mở đầu: Paraphrase đề — nêu rõ loại biểu đồ, đối tượng và thời gian. Không copy nguyên đề.
2
Overview bắt buộc: "Overall" + 2 xu hướng chính. Đây là phần quan trọng nhất của Task 1 — thiếu overview sẽ mất điểm Task Achievement.
3
Trích dẫn số liệu: Có đơn vị %, có so sánh ("just over half"). Dùng "by far the dominant mode" — collocation tốt.
4
Đa dạng cách diễn đạt: "a rise of eight percentage points", "almost doubled", "climbing", "fell marginally" — tránh lặp "increased/decreased".
5
Kết bài: Tóm tắt xu hướng chính, không thêm thông tin mới — đúng chuẩn Task 1.

Tránh mất điểm

6 lỗi thường gặp ở band 5.5

Sửa những lỗi này có thể giúp bạn tăng 0.5–1.0 band Writing ngay lập tức.

✗ In nowadays, people...
✓ Nowadays, people...

"Nowadays" đã là trạng từ chỉ thời gian, không cần giới từ "in" đứng trước.

✗ Many peoples / informations
✓ Many people / information

Danh từ không đếm được (information, advice, knowledge) không có dạng số nhiều.

✗ In my opinion, I think that...
✓ In my opinion, ... / I believe that...

Không dùng hai cụm nêu quan điểm liên tiếp — sẽ bị coi là lặp ý.

✗ The most biggest problem
✓ The biggest problem

Không dùng "most" trước tính từ đã ở dạng so sánh nhất.

✗ I very like this idea
✓ I really like this idea

"Very" không đứng trước động từ — dùng "really", "strongly", "particularly".

✗ There are many reason why...
✓ There are many reasons why...

Lỗi chia số nhiều — "many" luôn đi với danh từ đếm được số nhiều.
